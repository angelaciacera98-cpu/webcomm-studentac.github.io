Recap: Refactoring the home page of CSBNO

By Angela Ciacera 7954IA

WHAT IS CSBNO?

My page is about Web Opac of CSBNO libraries. CSBNO (Culture Socialità Biblioteche Network Operativo, formerly Consorzio Sistema Bibliotecario Nord Ovest) is a special consortium that coordinates over 60 libraries in 32 municipalities in the north-west of Milan.

WHY REFACTORING THE PAGE

  • I wanted to modify the home page because I think that it is too old, with an old layout and graphic and its too complicated to understand. You don’t see at first what you are really looking for, like the catalogue or the information about libraries, their contacts or their location. There is information that you have to find on yourself inside the home page but it takes too long.

At first in my home page you see the coockie that ask you to log in in your page. I think its useful for whom is searching a book and what to book it in a library, because its already logged in. Also who wants to look up for its own catalogue.
